This comprehensive review synthesizes recent advances in computer vision for cattle health and welfare monitoring, covering body condition scoring, lameness detection, weight estimation, estrus detection, feeding and drinking behavior, respiration monitoring, and general behavior analysis. The review examines camera systems, deep learning models, and multimodal sensing approaches while highlighting current challenges, research gaps, and future opportunities for deploying intelligent, non-invasive livestock monitoring systems in precision livestock farming.
