July 2026 — AI-Driven Wheat Yield Prediction Demo

I presented an interactive wheat yield prediction demonstration developed by the Group of Artificial Intelligence in Agriculture (G.A.I.A.) at Texas A&M AgriLife Research. The demonstration illustrated how artificial intelligence can integrate phenotypic, environmental, management, and genotypic data to predict wheat yield and support data-driven crop improvement and agricultural decision-making.

July 29, 2026 — Featured by the Texas A&M Postdoctoral Association

I was featured in the TAMU Postdoctoral Association Executive Committee Spotlight as Treasurer. The spotlight highlights my research in artificial intelligence for precision agriculture, my service to the postdoctoral community, and my role in supporting PDA activities.
